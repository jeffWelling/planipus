# Interface direction

Three directions were compared before markup was written.

| Direction | Character | Strength | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Observatory | Near-black operations console, luminous event lanes, dense live telemetry | Excellent for administrators and solver inspection | Too severe for daily personal planning |
| Transit board | White/black modular grid, route colors, timetable typography | Calendar relationships are instantly legible | Can feel rigid and municipal |
| Working almanac **(selected)** | Warm paper, ink-blue rules, bookish headings, stamped status labels | Calm, distinctive, and supports dense weekly decisions | Requires disciplined spacing to avoid nostalgia |

## Selected system: Working almanac

- **Canvas:** warm off-white `#f3efe4`, not pure white.
- **Ink:** `#15263b`; **risk:** `#c94d32`; **protected time:** `#99c2ad`;
  **task:** `#e5b85c`; **fixed event:** `#7894b0`.
- **Type:** local book-serif and mono fallbacks; no remote fonts or trackers.
- **Shape:** square cards, thin rules, small-radius controls. The Planipus/Pip
  mark should be a simple project-owned asset rather than a generated raster logo.
- **Motion:** limited to schedule blocks moving into a preview state. Reduced
  motion is respected.
- **Density:** week is primary; backlog and plan impact remain visible together.
- **Language:** "preview", "apply", "protected", "at risk", and "why here" are
  preferred over anthropomorphizing the solver.

Accessibility target: WCAG 2.2 AA, full keyboard operation, visible focus,
patterns/text in addition to color, and a useful narrow-screen agenda view.
