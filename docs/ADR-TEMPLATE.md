# ADR-NNN — Short decision title

Status: proposed | accepted | rejected | superseded

Date: YYYY-MM-DD

Owners/reviewers:

Requirements/risks:

## Context

What changed, constraints, current evidence, and why a decision is needed now.
Separate facts from assumptions. Link source/test/evidence without secrets.

## Decision drivers

- driver and relative importance;
- compatibility/security/privacy/operations;
- user/contributor impact;
- cost of delay and reversibility.

## Options

### Option A

Description, advantages, disadvantages, risks, migration, exit strategy.

### Option B

Description, advantages, disadvantages, risks, migration, exit strategy.

## Decision

Chosen option and exact scope. Include pinned versions/protocols/thresholds.

## Consequences

Positive, negative, follow-up work, tests/metrics/runbook/docs, deprecation and
rollback boundary.

## Validation/revisit trigger

Evidence needed before/after implementation and objective triggers to revisit.

## Supersedes / superseded by

Links to earlier/later ADRs. Never delete superseded reasoning.
